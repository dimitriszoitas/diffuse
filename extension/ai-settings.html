How AI review works

Screenshots and billing

An AI review sends the production screenshot, the prototype screenshot in comparison mode, and your review instructions to your selected AI provider. API usage is billed to that account.

Your key stays separate

Each key is used only by Diffuse’s connection to that provider. It is never added to comments, exported reports, or the pages you are reviewing.
